Cookie Policy

A short, straightforward explanation of cookies, how this website behaves, and the controls available in your browser.

Last updated · 6 October 2026

No trackingNo analytics, advertising or social-media cookies are used on this website.
Essential onlyThe hosting service may set strictly necessary cookies to keep the site secure and available.
You stay in controlYour browser settings let you view, block or delete cookies at any time.

What is a cookie, really?

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. The next time your browser talks to that website, it can send the cookie back, which lets the site recognise the browser or remember a choice.

Similar tools exist too — such as local storage and session storage inside the browser. On this page, "cookies" refers to all of these together.

Does this website set cookies?

Not by design. introarchitecture.it.com is a simple, static website. Its own code does not create cookies, does not store anything in local storage, and does not load analytics, advertising or social-media scripts.

The menu, the header and the scrolling effects run entirely in your browser while the page is open and keep no record once you leave.

Our web hosting provider may set a strictly necessary cookie for technical reasons — for example, to balance traffic between servers or to protect the site against malicious requests. Such cookies do not identify you personally and are not used for marketing.

Because no preference, functionality, analytics or advertising cookies are used, the website does not show a cookie consent banner.

What about the email link?

Clicking an email address on this website simply opens your own email program. It does not set a cookie. Once you send a message, it is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

How can I control cookies in my browser?

Every modern browser lets you see, block and delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies on any website may stop some parts of it from working correctly. You will usually find the controls here:

Chrome
Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies / Site data
Safari
Settings (Preferences) → Privacy → Manage Website Data
Firefox
Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
Edge
Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
Mobile browsers
Look under the browser's privacy or site settings menu
Private browsing
Clears cookies automatically when the private window is closed

Will this ever change?

If we add a feature that relies on cookies — for example, remembering a display preference — we will update this page first, describe the cookie and its purpose, and ask for consent where the law requires it. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
