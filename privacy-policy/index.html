Our approach in plain words

We collect as little as we can, we use it only to reply to you and to run this website, and we never sell it.

This notice explains how Intro Architecture ("we", "us") handles personal information connected with introarchitecture.it.com. It applies to visitors browsing the site and to anyone who writes to us by email.

Details you choose to send us

The website has no sign-up, account area or online form. The main way personal information reaches us is when you decide to email us. A message might include:

  • your name and email address;
  • a phone number or other contact details you add;
  • information about your property or space, such as floor plans, measurements or photographs;
  • anything else you choose to tell us about your project, preferences or timing.

Please only send what you are comfortable sharing. If floor plans or photos show other people, personal documents or sensitive details, consider removing them before sending.

Information your browser shares automatically

Like nearly every website, this one is delivered by a web server. When your browser requests a page, the server may record technical details in routine logs — for example your IP address, browser type, the page requested, the referring page and the date and time.

These logs are used to keep the site running, to diagnose errors and to protect against misuse. We do not run analytics software, advertising tags or tracking pixels on this website, and we do not use these logs to build profiles of individual visitors.

Why we use what we receive

We use the information you send to:

  • read and respond to your message;
  • understand your space well enough to discuss interior planning with you;
  • keep a record of our correspondence;
  • meet any legal, accounting or regulatory duties that apply to us.

Where the law requires a basis for this, we rely on taking steps at your request before any agreement, our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us, or compliance with legal obligations.

Who else may see it

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. It may be handled by a limited number of service providers who help us operate — such as our email and website hosting providers — only so far as needed to provide those services.

If you later ask us to work with other professionals on your project, such as a contractor or furniture maker, we will only share relevant details with your agreement. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep correspondence

Emails are kept for as long as they are useful for the conversation or project they relate to, and afterwards for a reasonable period in case questions arise. Information we must retain for legal or accounting reasons is kept for the period those rules require. When it is no longer needed, it is deleted.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable practical steps to protect the information you send, including limiting who can access it and using reputable service providers. The website is delivered over an encrypted connection. Even so, no method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate;
  • delete information we no longer need;
  • restrict or object to certain uses;
  • provide a copy of information you gave us.

To make a request, email Support@introarchitecture.it.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting. You may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority where you live.

Children

This website is intended for adults planning interiors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal details, let us know and we will delete them.

Links to other sites

If this website ever links to another site, that site's own privacy practices apply once you leave introarchitecture.it.com. We are not responsible for how other websites handle information.

Updates to this notice

We may revise this notice when our practices or the law change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Reaching us about privacy

Questions about this notice or your information can be sent to Support@introarchitecture.it.com, or by post to [Business address].
